Bull / Bear "Market Mood" mascots
=================================

Drop two images in THIS folder to enable the animated trend mascots:

    bull.png   -> shown for an UPWARD trend  (gently bobs)
    bear.png   -> shown for a DOWNWARD trend (gently sways)

Recommended:
  - PNG with a TRANSPARENT background (so it sits cleanly on the tinted card)
  - Roughly square, ~256-512px
  - A detailed 3D-rendered bull and bear look great here

Until both files exist, the Trend & Technical section just shows the plain
"Overall trend: ..." text — nothing breaks.

(Reminder: Windows may hide file extensions. If your file shows as
"bull.png" but the app can't find it, it may really be "bull.png.png" or
".jpg". Turn on View > Show > File name extensions to check.)
